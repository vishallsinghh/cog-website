ABOUT US
Established in June 1987, The
Council of Gujarat K.S.I. Jamaats was
created with a simple but enduring
purpose: to bring the community
together and contribute meaningfully
to its development and wellbeing.
For nearly four decades, COG has
worked across Gujarat to support
communities through education,
housing, healthcare, welfare, higher
education, social development,
group marriages and emergency
relief.
Our strength lies in collective action
connecting Jamaats, leaders,
volunteers and supporters around
shared needs and shared
responsibility.
