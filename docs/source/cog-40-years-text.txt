WHO WE ARE
The Council of Gujarat (COG) is a community institution serving the Khoja Shia Ithna-
Asheri Jamaats of Gujarat.
Established in June 1987, COG was created with a clear purpose: to contribute to the
welfare, development and wellbeing of the community, with particular attention to
women and children and their physical, mental and educational development. 
Over the years, that purpose has grown into a broad programme of community
service spanning education, higher education, housing, medical assistance, welfare,
social development, group marriages and relief during times of hardship and natural
calamity.
Today, COG's work reaches across more than 100 cities and villages in Gujarat,
bringing Jamaats, community leadership, volunteers, donors and well-wishers
together around a shared responsibility: to serve our community and build a stronger
future for the generations that follow.

WHERE WE BEGAN
The story of the Council of Gujarat did not begin in isolation in 1987.
For years, the KSI community in Gujarat had recognised the need for collective action
to respond to poverty, strengthen education, improve housing, support families in
difficulty and create opportunities for a more secure future.
The archival record of the period preceding COG reflects a growing tradition of
organised community development across Kutch and Kathiawad, including initiatives
in housing, education, agriculture, employment, medical assistance, relief and group
marriages. The Gujarat Federation, established in 1979, played an important role in
this earlier phase of organised development. 
As the needs of the community grew, so did the need for a dedicated institution that
could bring the Jamaats of Gujarat together around a common platform.
THE ROOTS OF COLLECTIVE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT
